# Changes in 2.6.0

## Added

- A form funnel on the Reports screen shows how many people saw, started and sent each form and the last field people touched before leaving without sending it, with a plain suggestion for each field; it keeps only daily totals per form, sets no cookies, stores no answers or IP addresses, skips robots and people who manage SubtleForms, and follows the Count form views switch. Form views are no longer counted for people who manage SubtleForms either, so Seen and Started count the same visitors.
- Settings, Tools, Migrate finds the forms you made with Contact Form 7, Gravity Forms or Fluent Forms on this site and imports the ones you tick as drafts, with their fields, required rules, choices, email notifications and thank-you message, and a report of anything that could not be copied; entries and payment settings are not imported and the original forms are not changed. Email notifications have a Reply-To setting that takes one address or a field tag such as {field:email}, and imported forms keep their Reply-To.
- New forms now stop spam robots quietly without a CAPTCHA, because the hidden trap field and the too-fast check are on by default for every form you create, the dashboard shows how many spam messages were blocked in the last 30 days and why, each form shows its own count under Spam protection, and sites that update get a one-time offer to turn both on for all their forms with Undo.

## Changed

- Forms load faster: every page with a form now downloads 22 percent less SubtleForms code (46 KB instead of 59 KB compressed), the date picker, phone and country lists, uploads, payments and the conversational layout are only downloaded by forms that use them, pages without a form still load nothing, and on a slow mobile connection a form is ready about a quarter of a second sooner.
- The SubtleForms screens no longer pin the WordPress admin menu in its own scrolling column on short windows; the menu behaves as in WordPress.
- The Submissions screen no longer has a New tab, because All already lists unread entries; an old link to the New tab opens All.
- Tools is now a tab in Settings, and Settings has an MCP tab (Pro adds its screen; without Pro it explains MCP and links to the upgrade page). The old Tools address opens the Tools tab.

## Fixed

- The Submissions footer (result count, previous and next, rows per page) stays at the bottom of the list when a tab or a search has no results.
- Submissions lists past the first 100 entries when 50 or 100 rows per page are chosen, and an empty page after the last row was removed keeps its real page count so Previous works.
- Choosing 50 or 100 rows per page on the Submissions screen lists the entries instead of showing "Entries could not be loaded".
