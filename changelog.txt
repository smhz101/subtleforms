=== SubtleForms changelog ===

This file keeps the full release history. readme.txt lists only the latest versions.

== Changelog ==

= 2.4.0 - 2026-10-20 =
* New: A payment form has its own currency setting under Settings, Payments. It follows the site currency until you pick one.
* New: More currencies for payments, including JPY, KRW, INR, PKR, AED, SAR, SGD, HKD, CHF, NZD, SEK, NOK, DKK, PLN, MXN, BRL and ZAR.
* Fix: Saving a form's Payments settings no longer fixes the form to US dollars, so the site currency applies.
* Fix: The Custom amount field shows the form's currency symbol on the live form and in the builder.
* Fix: Yen and other currencies without cents are rounded to whole units in prices, the summary, the charge and the entry.
* Fix: Product and priced option answers in entries, emails and exports show whole units for every currency without cents, such as KRW, not only for JPY.
* Fix: Entries, CSV exports and resent emails show prices in the currency the entry was charged in, so changing the site or form currency later does not relabel older entries.
* Fix: A coupon typed in a Coupon field, or with spaces in it, gives the same total at payment and on submit.
* Fix: A coupon with an unreadable date, or an expiry before its start, is refused with a message.
* Fix: Order quotes, coupon checks and payment intents answer not found for a draft form, like submitting does.
* Fix: Product name, price and quantity settings are under General in the builder.
* Fix: Priced options show no choice as selected on the builder canvas unless one is set as the default.
* Fix: Entries whose payment failed show Payment failed in the entries list and on the dashboard.
* Fix: With SubtleForms Pro active and no payment gateway connected, admins are asked to connect Stripe.
* Fix: Payment validation messages can be translated.
* Fix: An order below the smallest card charge for its currency gets a clear message before and at payment.
* Fix: Submissions exported and imported in Tools keep their order, coupon, totals, payment status, gateway and transaction ID, and a failed payment stays failed.
* Fix: Priced options shown as a dropdown use the same picker as other dropdowns, with a name and required state.
* Change: An entry refers to the form version it was sent with instead of storing a full copy of the form, about 1 KB, in its meta. Entry lists no longer carry a form copy. A background job removes the copies older entries hold, a few hundred entries at a time. An older entry whose form version cannot be found keeps its copy, so it stays readable.
* Fix: One formatter turns answers into text for the entry view, emails, CSV exports, PDF data and integrations, so they all read the same.
* Fix: Dropdown, radio, checkbox, picture and priced option answers show their labels. Single checkbox, consent and toggle answers read Yes or No everywhere, including the PDF.
* Fix: Matrix answers name each row and its chosen columns, for example "Speed: Good; Price: OK" and "Mon: AM, PM".
* Fix: Date, time and date and time answers follow the field's display format in the entry view, emails, CSV exports and PDFs.
* Fix: Dependent dropdown answers show the label chosen at each level in emails, PDFs, CRM notes and webhooks, including the full payload of a Webhook action, and in mapped integration fields when the add-on passes the form.
* Fix: Product answers read as order lines with a quantity and price, and priced options show their label and price.
* Fix: CSV exports list uploaded files by name with an admin download link instead of the stored file record.
* Fix: A field with a very long label and no admin label is named by its label shortened to 60 characters in emails, CSV headers, entries, PDFs and the required message.
* Fix: The submissions list names the sender by a name or email answer, or shows Anonymous. A product quantity or a choice is never shown as the sender.
* Fix: The server checks every answer the way the form does. A choice must be one the field offers; dates, times, ratings, scores, sliders, matrix answers, colours and signatures must be real values; a field that takes one value refuses a list; and a read-only field keeps its value.
* Fix: Server error messages use the same words as the form and can be translated.
* Fix: An email field that allows several addresses accepts them separated by commas and stores each one. With RFC validation off, an address such as user@localhost is accepted.
* Fix: A website can be typed without https://, which is added. Only http and https addresses are accepted, in the form and on the server.
* Fix: Pasting an international phone number picks its country, the leading 0 of a national number is dropped, and the server checks the calling code and the length. A +1 number with a Canadian area code shows Canada.
* Fix: A Custom amount outside its minimum or maximum, a Product quantity above its maximum and a Priced option that is not offered are refused, instead of being charged a different amount. A pick-one Priced options field charges one option.
* Fix: A required Rich text field with no text in it is refused.
* Fix: A Repeater with fewer entries than its minimum shows one message, such as "Add at least 2 entries.", instead of errors for fields that were never shown.
* Fix: Hidden field values, including {date}, {user_email} and {user_name}, are worked out on the server, so they are stored when a form is sent without JavaScript.
* Fix: Values sent for an Order summary or Fixed fee field are not stored, so they no longer appear as columns in CSV exports.
* Fix: A builder save that fails names the field as well as the setting, a numeric label, placeholder or default value saves, and long labels wrap on the canvas.
* Fix: A column stored as an object with a fields or children list renders its fields on the live form, includes them in the live total, validates them and gives them defaults, so a product in that column is shown as well as charged.
* Fix: Deleting a field inside a step, container or column shows the Field deleted notice with Undo, and deleting a step counts every field in it.
* Fix: When the forms list fails to load after a change of tab, search, sort or page size, the previous rows are cleared, so Published forms are not shown or actionable under Trash. A failed retry of the same view keeps its rows.
* Fix: When the entries list fails to load after a change of tab, search, date range, form or sort, the previous entries are cleared, so Inbox entries are not shown or actionable under Trash. A failed retry of the same view keeps its entries.
* Fix: In the builder Preview, a desktop form wider than the canvas is scaled into a stage of the scaled size, centred in the canvas, so the whole form stays visible beside the styling panel with no sideways scroll.
* Fix: The Payment Form template's product is set as a fixed product: no quantity box in the builder or on the live form, and the quote, the payment intent and the entry charge it once without a submitted value.
* Fix: The Donation Form template's Custom Amount is required and has a minimum of 1, in the browser and on the server, so an empty or zero amount cannot complete a donation form. A donation template from an add-on gets the same rule unless it sets a higher minimum.
* Fix: Templates from add-ons lose only raw card inputs, found by an id or key such as card_number, expiry or cvv. A field or container whose label or id only mentions a card, such as a Gift card message or a Scorecard, is kept, and so is the Card Payment field.
* Fix: A payment template from an add-on that already prices the order with Priced options, a Custom Amount or a Fixed fee no longer gets a 10.00 fixed product added and charged on top. In a multi-step template, a missing product goes in the first step and a missing Order summary and note go in the last step, instead of beside the steps.
* Fix: The server checks conditional logic against the answers as typed, before a website gets https:// and before email lists and phone numbers are tidied, as the browser does. A rule such as Website equals example.com keeps the fields, priced fields, conditional emails and confirmations it controls. The stored answers are still tidied.
* Fix: The live form refuses a Product quantity that is not a whole number, is below the minimum (including the starting 1 when the minimum is higher) or is above the maximum, with the server's messages, before any card payment is started.
* Fix: A color field saves the color it shows, including its default.
* Fix: Each CAPTCHA field uses its own provider and keys, and the form says when a check is not set up.
* Fix: Password fields can show a strength meter and ask for the password again.
* Fix: A US phone number is grouped as it is typed, and a short number is rejected.
* Fix: Dropdown, radio and multiple choice start with choices, and the canvas shows the default choice.
* Fix: Choice groups are not tied to a missing control, prefix and suffix text is named for screen readers, and a consent link uses the words already in the sentence.
* Fix: A section heading and custom HTML each take a full row, and column layouts use a real column count.
* Fix: Hidden and color fields no longer repeat the admin label or the default value.
* Fix: Net Promoter Score has a low and a high label, and a Calculation field has a prefix and a suffix.
* Fix: An Action Hook field runs when the form is shown and when it is submitted.
* Fix: Logic for a checkbox, toggle or country offers the real values, and an off toggle counts as empty.
* Fix: The second dependent dropdown lists only the choices that belong to the first answer, and the first dropdown keeps its label.
* Fix: Replacing dependent dropdowns from a file asks first, and a semicolon file imports as separate columns.
* Fix: Dropdown, country, phone and date fields work from the keyboard. The calendar can jump to a month and year, and country buttons show a letter code.
* Fix: The first invalid dropdown, country, date or phone control receives focus.
* Fix: The admin and confirmation email switches apply to every form, and the saved sender is used when an email has no From address.
* Fix: Submitting the form preview does not send email or webhooks, does not count toward the entry limit, and is only available to an administrator.
* Fix: A form block that is not using AJAX submits with a normal page load.
* Fix: A form block that is not using AJAX shows the errors and keeps the answers when a submission is refused, shows the confirmation message once it is accepted, and follows a redirect confirmation only after a successful submission.
* Fix: A form block that is not using AJAX follows a confirmation redirect to another site, as the AJAX form does, instead of sending visitors to the home page.
* Fix: Confirmation messages and redirects fill merge tags.
* Fix: A custom webhook body fills merge tags.
* Fix: Resend says when the email was not queued, and sending it again still sends.
* Fix: Removing a flag or spam label restores the entry status, including a payment status.
* Fix: Delete follows the Trash or Hard setting for forms and entries.
* Fix: The New count follows the form you are viewing, and opening an entry marks it read in the list.
* Fix: Dashboard counts refresh when an entry is saved.
* Fix: Dashboard and reports count from midnight in the site timezone.
* Fix: Autosave uses the interval in Settings, and turning it off stops it.
* Fix: A link to an entry loads that entry even when it is past the first page. The list keeps its tab and search, and Back returns to the list.
* Fix: Confirmation rows use the same spacing as the rest of the builder.
* Fix: A form in a block is the same width as the same form in a shortcode.
* Fix: A theme-styled form uses the theme button, the theme font and the width of the content column, and the builder preview loads the theme fonts.
* Fix: Publishing a form no longer looks unpublished when the only difference is the order of appearance settings.
* Fix: Gravity Forms and Fluent Forms imports keep pages, hidden fields, names and conditional logic, and leave payment, quiz and post fields out.
* Fix: On Tools, choosing a file is one keyboard stop, notices clear when you change section, and forms with the same title show their number and status.
* Fix: Importing entries warns when a file is from another form and skips entries that are already there. The transfer screen says notes, the IP address, the browser, files, logs and unpublished drafts are not in the file.
* Fix: A webhook that is still waiting says Queued.

* Fix: Deleting a form deletes its entries, uploaded files, activity logs, schema versions and view totals. The retention purge removes logs too.
* Fix: The personal data export and erase tools match the exact email address and show the form name.

* Fix: The API log skips unknown routes and visitor traffic, and is capped. The submit route has its own rate limit.
* Fix: A numeric HubSpot Portal ID no longer blocks saving Settings.
* Fix: Uploads use the WordPress upload and filesystem functions, and new sites store files in a folder with a random name.
* Fix: Name and Address part settings are kept, drawn and checked the same way in the browser and on the server.
* Fix: Notice, Section heading and Page break settings show in the builder, and a step shows its description.
* Fix: Files uploaded inside a Repeater are kept with the entry and linked in the entry and email.
* Fix: Dependent dropdown values come from their labels, a new choice never reuses an old value, the Columns layout does not pre-fill an answer, and every level of a required dependent field is required.
* Fix: Priced fields hidden by conditional logic are not charged, and a hidden Fixed fee is not charged or listed.
* Fix: For card payments, emails and integrations run only after the payment goes through. A failed payment emails the site owner instead.
* Fix: The builder notes when a priced form has no Card Payment field and will save entries as Payment pending.
* Fix: Every template emails the site owner instead of posting to an example address. The Payment form has a product and an order summary, without card number fields, and the Donation form has a custom amount.
* Fix: The builder header, preview, conversational choices, coupon field and presets fit and stay readable. Failed loads and saves show Retry. The builder shows a placeholder while loading. Deleting a field can be undone. Quick Start creates the form. Last 7 days matches on the dashboard and Reports.

* Fix: The AI assistant settings tab is removed. It reported a successful connection for any key and blocked saving other settings.
* Change: SubtleForms requires WordPress 6.6.
* Fix: Deleting the plugin removes its tables, options, transients, uploads and scheduled tasks on every site in a network.
* Fix: Text in the admin and on the public form can be translated with a language pack.

= 2.3.7 - 2026-10-18 =

* Fix: A field label can be a short paragraph. A consent line longer than 255 characters now saves. The limit is 2,000 characters, and a save error names the field label when it is too long.

= 2.3.2 - 2026-10-13 =
* Fix: Creating a form from some templates opened an empty form, for example Volunteer Signup, Event Registration and Vendor Application in SubtleForms Pro. Those templates used a single checkbox with a list of options, which the server refused, and the refusal was not reported. Such a checkbox is now saved as a Checkboxes (Multiple choice) field, every template creates its full form with its fields, settings, notifications and type, and a form that cannot be created shows the reason and leaves no empty form behind.
* Fix: The form type follows the form. Adding the first Page Break to a Standard form makes it Multi-step, and removing the last one makes it Standard again with every field kept. The builder badge, the Settings tab, All Forms, the dashboard, the entry details and the live form all show the same type, and a message with Undo tells you when the type changes. Forms saved by older versions are corrected when the plugin updates.
* New: A Form type setting on the Settings tab. Changing a Standard form to Multi-step puts every field in a first step. Changing a Multi-step form to Standard or Conversational removes the steps and keeps every field in order, after you confirm.
* Fix: A new conversational form now starts with "Add your first question" instead of a block that could not be edited or removed. Every question, a welcome screen too, can be edited, replaced with another field, moved earlier or later, duplicated and deleted. Clicking a field in the panel fills an empty question, and otherwise adds a new question right after the one you are on. Add question makes an empty question where you are.
* Fix: The live conversational form asks the same questions in the same order as the builder shows, says "Question 2 of 5", treats a Group or Repeater as one question and checks each question with the same rules and messages as other forms.
* Fix: The label and help text of a Repeater, Group and Columns can now be edited in their settings. They are saved and show in the builder and on the live form, with the container's own settings such as the Repeater button label and limits.
* New: Every field has a Field label visibility setting. Visible shows the label above the field. Hidden hides it from view but keeps it for screen readers, so the field still has a name. This replaces the Hide label option of the Style tab, and fields that used it keep their hidden label.
* New: Every field with help text has a Help text placement setting, below or above the input, on the builder canvas and on the live form.
* New: Every input field has a Custom validation message, shown when an answer is filled in but not valid, for example a wrong email address, a number out of range or text that does not match the pattern. It replaces the pattern message of text fields. Every input field also has the message for when it is left empty, and the server uses both messages too.
* New: Field defaults on the Settings tab set the label visibility, help text placement and both messages for every field that does not set its own.
* Fix: The Admin label of a field is now used everywhere it should be: entries, notification emails ({all_fields}), CSV export, the PDF and data sent to integrations, webhooks and Zapier. Hidden fields and Repeaters have an Admin label too.
* Fix: Group and Columns fields, which are no longer in the field library, showed no settings in the builder. Their settings are back.
* Fix: Custom HTML fields show their content on the builder canvas instead of a placeholder.
* Developer: New helpers SchemaRepair::checkboxListsToMultipleChoice(), SchemaRepair::syncType() and FieldMapper::adminLabel(). FieldMapper::fields() rows carry the admin label as "label" and the public label as "public_label". Form metadata accepts labelVisibility, descriptionPlacement, requiredMessage and validationMessage.
* Database: When the plugin updates, the type stored with each form is set from its structure (Multi-step when it has a Page Break, Standard when it has none). Conversational forms are not changed. No tables or columns are added.

= 2.3.1 - 2026-10-12 =
* Fix: Adding a Page Break to a form hid every existing field behind a new empty step. A Page Break now splits the form where it is placed: the fields above it become step 1 and the fields below it the next step. Added at the very top, the existing fields stay in step 1 and the break starts an empty step 2 after them. Undo and Redo reverse the whole change in one step.
* Fix: Forms saved by older versions with fields outside every step are repaired when they are opened in the builder and when the live form loads. Those fields move into the first step with their order, keys, settings and conditions kept, and the builder says so until you save.
* New: Every field of a multi-step form has a Step setting in its General settings. Choosing another step moves the field to the end of that step, keeps its key, settings, conditions and payment links, switches the canvas to that step and keeps the field selected. You can also drag a field onto a step tab.
* Fix: Repeaters now work for visitors. Add and Remove buttons use your button label and respect the minimum and maximum number of groups. Each group has its own inputs, names (for example companies[0][ticker]), required checks, errors and conditions, focus moves to the new group or the next one after a removal, and the buttons have clear names for screen readers. This works on the live form, in the builder Preview and in the block editor preview, and on phones.
* Fix: The server checks every Repeater group on its own and stores the answer as a list of groups. Entries show the groups as a table, emails list them under {all_fields} (a table in HTML emails), and the PDF data used by SubtleForms Pro includes each group.
* Change: CSV export gives a Repeater one column per child field, named "Repeater label: Child label". Each group is one line inside the cell, in the same order in every column of that Repeater, so line 2 of every column belongs to group 2. Exports that mix several forms write each group as a numbered line such as "1. ticker: AAPL; shares: 8".
* Fix: Fields could not be dragged into a Repeater, Group or columns because pressing anywhere on the container started dragging the container. Containers now accept drops into their drop zone, only the container handle drags the container, the zone that will take the drop is highlighted (also inside nested containers), and fields can be dragged in and out of containers and from the field library. The new Move to menu on each field toolbar does the same from the keyboard.
* Fix: Clicking a field inside a Repeater, Group or columns, or pressing Enter on it, opened the container's settings. It now opens the field's own Settings, Validation, Logic and Style, and the container is still selected from its header.
* Fix: The Trash tab of All Forms showed trashed forms as Draft with the normal actions. They now show as Trashed with only Restore and Delete permanently (which asks first), in the row menu and as bulk actions. Restore returns a form to the status it had before, and tab counts refresh. A trashed form does not show or accept entries.
* Fix: Duplicating a form failed for multi-step forms with an empty step. Duplicate now runs on the server in one request for any form, including multi-step, Repeater and payment forms. The copy gets a new ID, Draft status and the title with " (copy)", no entries are copied, and a failure shows the actual reason and leaves no half-made copy.
* Fix: A new blank form starts with a working admin notification sent to {admin_email} with the subject "New entry: {form_title}". An email action without a recipient or subject shows "Needs setup", its warnings only show while it is switched on, and it never blocks saving.
* Fix: A payment form priced by Product or Priced option fields failed every autosave with "Unsaved changes" and "Retry autosave", because saving required a fixed amount or an amount field and dropped the checkout settings. Such forms now save, and a failed save names the setting at fault.
* Fix: Save and validation notices of a form are cleared when you leave the form, and no longer follow you to other screens.
* New: Entries Trash. Deleting an entry, alone or in bulk, moves it to a new Trash tab where it can be restored to its previous status or deleted permanently. The Trash is emptied automatically after 30 days by the daily cleanup; change the number of days under Settings > Advanced. Entries in the Trash are left out of counts, limits, exports, analytics and the dashboard, and cannot be resent.
* Change: The Number, Slider and Payment Amount fields call their increment setting "Step size", so it is not confused with form steps.
* Developer: New REST routes POST /forms/{id}/duplicate and POST /submissions/{id}/restore. DELETE /submissions/{id} moves the entry to the Trash unless force=1 is passed. New actions subtleforms/form/duplicated, subtleforms/submission/trashed, subtleforms/submission/untrashed and subtleforms/schema/repaired_orphans. FieldMapper::answers() rows for a Repeater carry a "groups" list for renderers that draw a table.
* Database: Adds a trashed_from column to the forms table and trashed_from and trashed_at columns to the entries table. They are added automatically when the plugin updates; no data is changed.

= 2.3.0 - 2026-10-11 =
* New: Screens for SubtleForms Pro 2.3.0: an integration log with Retry on each entry, a webhook delivery log, "Send test contact" in the form integrations and a custom support link for white label.
* New: Email merge tags. An "Insert tag" menu in the email settings adds {all_fields}, {field:key}, {form_title}, {entry_id}, {site_name}, {admin_email} and {date} at the cursor. Single and double braces both work, so {{all_fields}} and forms made before 2.3 keep working. The help menu explains every tag.
* New: Emails can be sent as HTML, with line breaks kept and {all_fields} drawn as a table. An empty message now sends every answer instead of raw data.
* New: Newsletter template with one email field and the button on the same row, an inline field layout for any form, and a Dark surface appearance preset for footers and hero sections. Previews show dark-surface forms on a dark background.
* New: Entries can be starred, with a Starred tab. Bulk actions for read, unread, star, spam, flag, delete and export selected use one request, and bulk delete asks first.
* New: Private notes on each entry, with author and date.
* New: Resend notifications from an entry, using the answers as they were saved.
